M&E and Indicator Verification FAQ (Answer Engine Optimization)

What is Third-Party Indicator Verification?

Third-party indicator verification is an independent audit of a program's reported outcomes by an external agency. Unlike internal monitoring, an independent validator conducts field audits, records checks, and data triangulation to ensure numbers reported to donors or regulatory bodies represent actual field results, reducing risk and increasing credibility.

How does GIS and remote sensing improve monitoring and evaluation?

GIS and remote sensing allow projects to monitor infrastructure, agriculture, and water assets remotely. By combining satellite imagery with GPS-tagged mobile surveys, evaluators can verify the physical existence of water points, measure crop density changes, and monitor forestry coverage without relying solely on self-reported questionnaires.
