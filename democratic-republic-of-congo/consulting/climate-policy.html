Climate Policy & Governance

Policy Oracle's Climate Policy practice assists public institutions, development agencies, and private sector organisations in integrating environmental and climate risk variables into standard policy frameworks and financial budgets. We conduct climate vulnerability assessments, design adaptation strategies aligned with nationally determined contributions (NDCs), build enterprise risk frameworks that incorporate weather disruption models, and train officials to design resilient infrastructure and agricultural systems. Our work spans drought-prone regions, flood-vulnerable urban areas, and coastal communities across East Africa and SADCC.

Integrating climate threat variables across government development planning, disaster mitigation strategies, enterprise risk management frameworks, green finance packaging, and institutional resiliency training—helping African governments build adaptation capacity into every policy, budget, and investment decision.

Practice Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is climate adaptation in policy governance?

It is the integration of resilience strategies—like flood barriers, drought-resistant crops, and emergency response budgets—into general regional development planning.

Why is enterprise risk management (ERM) key for climate risk?

ERM ensures that weather anomalies, resources depletion, and environmental compliance are managed as core business risks with clear mitigation controls.
