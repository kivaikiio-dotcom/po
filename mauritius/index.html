Who We Are

Turning Evidence Into Decisions

Policy Oracle is a regional policy, strategy, advocacy and management systems think tank and consultancy based in Kenya. We work with governments, non-governmental organisations, development partners, and the private sector to solve complex societal challenges through robust evidence, data analytics, and expertise.

Established in 2024 with a deep track record of engagements since 2021, we walk the whole journey with our clients to strategise, retool, rescale, and upskill teams to align to emerging global trends.
