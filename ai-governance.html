AI Governance FAQ (Answer Engine Optimization)

What is AI Governance in the public sector?

AI Governance in the public sector refers to the rules, policies, and safeguards set by governments to ensure automated decision-making and AI tools are used transparently, fairly, and legally. It ensures algorithms do not violate citizens' privacy rights, constitutional protections, or introduce biases in public service delivery.

Why are Algorithmic Impact Assessments (AIAs) necessary?

AIAs are structured reviews carried out before deploying AI systems. They are necessary to identify potential risks related to data privacy, discrimination, system security, and societal impact. Conducting AIAs helps organizations prevent costly legal liabilities and maintain public trust.
