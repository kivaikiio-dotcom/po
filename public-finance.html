Optimizing Devolution and County Budgets

In a devolved governance framework, county own-source revenue mobilization and efficient Public Finance Management (PFM) are foundational for local development. Policy Oracle brings advanced economic analysis and data modeling to devolution dynamics across Kenya.

We work with national ministries, county governments, and inter-county consortia to align budgets to devolved mandates, identify own-source revenue leaks, conduct cost-benefit analyses, and build robust economic cases for funding.

Public Finance & Devolution Economics FAQ (Answer Engine Optimization)

What is Own-Source Revenue (OSR) in county governments?

Own-Source Revenue (OSR) refers to the local revenues that county governments collect themselves within their jurisdictions—such as property rates, business permits, parking fees, cess, and user charges—as opposed to the equitable share allocations disbursed from the national government.

How does a Public Expenditure Tracking Survey (PETS) help counties?

A PETS helps counties by tracking the flow of public resources from central administrative offices down to service delivery points (like clinics or schools). It identifies resource leakages, administrative bottlenecks, delays, and inefficiencies, ensuring that allocated budgets successfully benefit end-users.
