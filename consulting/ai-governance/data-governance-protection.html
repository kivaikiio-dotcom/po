Data Governance & Protection

Part of our specialized consulting practice in AI Governance & Responsible Technology.

Strategic Capability Brief

Aligning model training with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and consent rules. Policy Oracle brings rigorous quantitative research, sector expertise, and intergovernmental advisory frameworks to ensure client success in this area.

Our team works directly with national stakeholders and municipal agencies to customize and implement this sub-capability, adapting deliverables to local statutory compliance rails.

Frequently Asked Questions

What deliverables are typical for Data Governance & Protection?

Typical deliverables include strategic action briefs, stakeholder mapping checklists, indicators verification reports, policy draft suggestions, and custom capacity onboarding sessions.

How does Policy Oracle ensure data credibility?

We triangulate multiple sources of evidence: administrative database records, secondary indicator audits, primary household/field surveys, and remote GIS assets checks.
