Practice Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What makes indicator verification independent?

Independence is achieved by separating the evaluation team from the implementation team, relying on cross-verified primary data, record triangulation, and strict audit methodologies.

What is a difference-in-differences design in M&E?

It is a statistical technique that compares the changes in outcomes over time between a treatment group and a control group, isolating the program impact from external factors.
