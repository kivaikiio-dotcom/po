Who We Are

About Policy Oracle

Strategy · Policy · Evidence · Impact

Policy Oracle is a regional policy, strategy, advocacy and management systems think tank and consultancy. We offer corporate support in research, data analytics, policy development and analysis, capacity development, business development, social policy, political strategy, economics and technology optimization.

With rapid changes in economic and social dynamics, technology and ever-evolving business situations, we help institutions strategise, retool, rescale, upskill or reskill, build capacities and innovate to meet the operational demands of the industry and align to emerging trends.

We tailor our programmes and products with today's and tomorrow's needs in mind and walk the whole journey with our clients. Our team has broad and deep experience working with diverse organisations on programme design, research, policy and strategy related challenges. We work with governments, non-governmental organisations, civil society, community based organisations, international development agencies and the private sector to solve society's challenges with evidence and expertise.

We bring experience across the full suite of policy development, regulation, data analytics, service design and delivery, funding and evaluation, to deliver impactful outcomes. We espouse high standards of probity and integrity, reflected in our professionalism, and our services are available on both onsite and virtual modules.

Above all, we exist to turn evidence into decisions, and decisions into results. That means being independent enough to tell clients what the data shows, practical enough to help them act on it, and committed enough to stay until the value is realised and the capability remains with the institution.

How We Are Governed

We hold high standards of probity and independence. Interests are declared openly, methods are documented so they can be scrutinised, and client information is handled with strict confidentiality. Our advice is guided by the evidence, not by what is easiest to hear.

Methodology

Our Approach

A disciplined, evidence-led method that carries clients from the underlying problem to a result they can sustain

01

Understand

We begin with the real problem, not the presenting symptom. We map stakeholders, incentives, context and constraints, and agree with the client on the exact decision the work must serve, so effort is grounded in the choice actually facing the institution.

02

Evidence

We collect and analyse robust qualitative and quantitative data using current tools and methods, from surveys and independent field verification to administrative data, economic modelling and structured evidence review, and we document everything so the analysis can be scrutinised.

03

Design

We craft human-centred, practical solutions and translate them into clear policy, strategy or programme design. Priorities are mapped, trade-offs are made explicit, and options are costed, so decision-makers can choose with their eyes open.

04

Deliver

We support implementation with actionable roadmaps, institutional arrangements, capacity building and knowledge systems, so that the client can carry the work forward without permanent dependence on us.

05

Evaluate

We assess impact before and after, measure what works and what does not, and feed the learning back into better decisions, treating evaluation as a source of improvement rather than a compliance exercise.

Methods and Tools We Draw On

Stakeholder and political-economy analysis · qualitative and quantitative research · survey and sampling design · independent field verification and record audits · remote sensing and GIS · econometric and statistical modelling · difference-in-differences and impact evaluation · cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness analysis · public expenditure and fiscal analysis · theory of change and results frameworks · data analytics and visualisation.
