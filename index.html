Who We Are

About Policy Oracle

A regional policy, strategy, advocacy and management systems think tank and consultancy

Policy Oracle is a regional policy, strategy, advocacy and management systems think tank and consultancy. We offer corporate support in research, data analytics, policy development and analysis, capacity development, business development, social policy, political strategy, economics and technology optimization.

With rapid changes in economic and social dynamics, technology and ever-evolving business situations, we help institutions strategise, retool, rescale, upskill or reskill, build capacities and innovate to meet the operational demands of the industry and align to emerging trends.

We work with governments, non-governmental organisations, civil society, community based organisations, international development agencies and the private sector to solve society's challenges with evidence and expertise.

Above all, we exist to turn evidence into decisions, and decisions into results. That means being independent enough to tell clients what the data shows, practical enough to help them act on it, and committed enough to stay until the value is realised.

How We Are Governed

We hold high standards of probity and independence. Interests are declared openly, methods are documented so they can be scrutinised, and client information is handled with strict confidentiality. Our advice is guided by the evidence, not by what is easiest to hear.

How We Work

Our Approach

A disciplined, evidence-led method that carries clients from the underlying problem to a result they can sustain

01

Understand

We begin with the real problem, not the presenting symptom. We map stakeholders, incentives, context and constraints, and agree with the client on the exact decision the work must serve.

02

Evidence

We collect and analyse robust qualitative and quantitative data using current tools and methods, from surveys and independent field verification to administrative data and economic modelling.

03

Design

We craft human-centred, practical solutions and translate them into clear policy, strategy or programme design. Priorities are mapped, trade-offs are made explicit, and options are costed.

04

Deliver

We support implementation with actionable roadmaps, institutional arrangements, capacity building and knowledge systems, so that the client can carry the work forward independently.

05

Evaluate

We assess impact before and after, measure what works and what does not, and feed the learning back into better decisions, treating evaluation as a source of improvement.

Flagship Practice

AI Governance and Responsible Technology

As AI moves into public services, revenue, health and finance, the question is no longer whether to adopt it, but how to adopt it responsibly, lawfully and in the public interest.

  • AI Policy and Regulatory Frameworks — Draft national and institutional AI strategies, policies and standards
  • AI Risk and Impact Assessment — Structured assessments of algorithmic, data protection, bias and rights-related risks
  • Data Governance and Protection — Align AI and data systems with data protection law and cross-border rules
  • Responsible AI Capacity Building — Equip officials, boards and regulators to procure, oversee and audit AI systems
  • AI Assurance and Audit — Independent review of models, vendors and deployments for transparency and fairness
  • Public-Sector AI Adoption Strategy — Identify high-value use cases and build governance rails for safe scaling
Typical Deliverables: AI policies and standards, risk and impact assessments, data governance frameworks, procurement guidelines, assurance reports, training curricula, adoption roadmaps.

Case Highlights

Health Economics · 2023

Cancer Screening Programme Economic and Business Case

National Cancer Institute

Built the economic and business case for the national cancer screening programme, measuring screening coverage, referral rates, and treatment initiation. Combined facility record audits, patient tracking and cost-benefit analysis to frame early detection as sound economics as well as sound public health.

Devolution Economics · 2023

National Economic Analysis of Devolution Outcomes

Ministry of Devolution and ASALs

Analysed the socio-economic effect of devolution using difference-in-differences, tracking county adoption of fiscal management reforms and modelling the fiscal gap closed. Gave national government rigorous, comparative evidence on what devolution delivers.

Independent Verification · 2025

Urban WASH Indicator Verification

WaterAid

Independently verified access to safely managed water in informal settlements, service interruptions, and sanitation coverage. Used utility data triangulation, water-quality testing, GIS mapping and customer feedback for trusted third-party evidence.

Agriculture M&E · 2023

County Climate-Resilient Agriculture M&E System

Machakos County Government

Designed the M&E system for the county's climate-resilient agriculture programme, tracking adoption of climate-smart practices, changes in crop yield per hectare, and farmer competency through field visits, remote sensing and training records.
